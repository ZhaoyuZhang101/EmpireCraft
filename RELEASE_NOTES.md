# 更新内容 (Update Features)

2026-10-22 · 0.4.7Beta1

新增战场音效与八系文明背景音乐。华夏选定国号重做，可开“姓氏国号”。帝国分裂改为预警、割据、争位内战；文明兴衰与各文明平衡重调。远距离不再宣战，久无接触自动议和；城墙挡军团，水师会登陆。换图层更快、全屏翻牌；营建新增绘制道路、连路神力。在线更新改为退出游戏时自动换上新版。模组设置改为分类窗口；宗族界面重做，妃嫔生育、宗室分支；厂区加古代工场，荒地少盖房。修复手机版读不进模组、无游牧处游牧化、城市忠诚卡顿、君主即位即老死、读外来旧图崩溃等。

Battle sounds and music for eight civilization families. Huaxia dynasty naming reworked, with an optional surname-naming law. Imperial breakups now pass through warnings, secession and succession wars; dynastic cycles and civilization balance are retuned. Distant realms no longer declare war, and wars without contact end in peace; walls block armies and navies land troops. Layer switches are faster and flip the whole view; new powers draw roads and connect cities. The online updater now installs updates when the game exits. Mod settings open in a categorized window, the clan window is rebuilt, harem consorts bear children and royal kin branch off; factory districts gain ancient workshops and barren land holds fewer houses. Fixes: mobile loading, manual titles during interregnums, nomadization with no nomads nearby, city loyalty stutter, rulers dying after accession, crashes on other mods' saves.
