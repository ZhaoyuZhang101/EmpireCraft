# 更新内容 (Update Features)

2026-10-22 · 0.4.7Beta1

新增战场音效与八系文明背景音乐，镜头拉近时缓缓放起，并接着上次的地方放。华夏选定国号重做，世界法则可开“姓氏国号”。帝国分裂改为预警、割据、争位内战的进程；文明兴衰周期与各文明平衡重调，西欧封建国家能长大。远距离不再宣战，久无接触的战争自动议和；城墙挡军团，水师会跨海登陆。换图层与进图改为边算边翻、不再空等；修复手机版读不进模组、色块不刷新、王国制度停滞、君主即位不久便老死、读其他模组旧图崩溃等问题。

Battle sounds and music for eight civilization families now fade in as the camera zooms in, resuming where they left off. Huaxia dynasty naming reworked, with an optional surname-naming world law. Imperial breakups now unfold through warnings, secession and succession wars; dynastic cycles and civilization balance are retuned, and Western feudal realms can grow. Distant realms no longer declare war, and wars without contact end in peace; walls block armies and navies launch landings. Layer switches and map entry now flip in as colors compute; fixes for the mobile version failing to load, stale map colors, stalled kingdom reforms, rulers dying soon after accession and crashes loading other mods' saves.
