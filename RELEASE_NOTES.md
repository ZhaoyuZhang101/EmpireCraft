# 更新内容 (Update Features)

2026-10-16 · 0.4.6Beta7

科技与制度强关联：新增典章科技线(律令法典、编户齐民、礼制、官僚文书、驿传、考选取士、宪政学说、大众政治等 22 项)，各文化的制度都挂上专门科技，没研究出来不能施行；立宪要先有印刷术，三省六部也要印刷；理念要靠书传开，本文化有该理念的奠基名著才会有人信奉。科技改为文化发明、国家采纳：本国(帝国按整个帝国)每年逐步采纳本文化的科技，图书馆、大学、城市越多越快，建筑、装备、兵种、改制、政体过渡都看本国是否已采纳；帝国窗口新增科技页。印刷分为造纸、雕版、活字、机械印刷四级，决定理念书籍的影响、能否外溢、新思潮能否在民间大规模传开；新增电报、广播、互联网，开放国家之间不接壤也会传入理念。扩张有了上限：控制范围看地形、科技、政体与君主，直辖容量早期很小(只能分封)；远方新征服之地管不到的转为羁縻州、诸侯、附庸或朝贡国，过度扩张时远方失稳、开战只求称臣。华夏补上原始体制：原始群→母系氏族→父系氏族→部落联盟→家天下→宗法，姓氏随之演变(无姓→女字旁古姓→贵族分氏→男子称氏、平民无氏→郡县后姓氏合一)，宗族与氏族合为姓—氏两层，原始阶段用上古人名与有熊、陶唐这类国号；文化配置可设开局阶段、姓氏演变与古姓词库。日本有了自己的制度线(绳文→弥生→大和→律令→摄关→幕府→明治)与幕府政体：将军执政、天皇虚君、大名世袭各藩，明治时大政奉还；旧存档按对应关系折算，不退回原始阶段。

Technology and institutions are now tightly linked: a new civics branch (22 techs such as law codes, census, bureaucracy, postal roads, examinations, constitutionalism and mass politics) gates every culture's institutions; constitutions and the Three Departments need printing, and ideologies only spread once their founding book is known. Technology is invented by the culture but adopted by each realm year by year (faster with libraries, universities and cities); buildings, equipment, units, reforms and regime changes require adoption, and empires get a technology tab. Printing now has four levels (paper, block printing, movable type, steam press) that govern how far ideas spread, and telegraph, radio and the internet let ideas reach open countries without a shared border. Expansion is capped: reach depends on terrain, technology, regime and ruler, direct-rule capacity starts small (early realms must enfeoff), distant new conquests become loose-rein prefectures, vassals or tributaries, and overextended realms suffer unrest and only seek submission in war. Huaxia gains prehistoric stages (bands, matrilineal and patrilineal clans, tribal alliance, hereditary kingship, then the Zhou clan order) with an evolving naming system from no surnames to ancient surnames, lineage names and finally merged surnames; lineage and clan become one surname-and-branch system, and early eras use archaic personal and tribal names. Culture configs can set the starting stage, surname evolution and ancient surname lists. Japan gets its own institution line (Jōmon to Meiji) and a Shogunate regime with a ruling shōgun, a figurehead emperor and hereditary daimyō domains, ending with the Meiji restoration; old saves are converted without regressing.
