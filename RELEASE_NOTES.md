## 更新内容(Update Features)

1. 重构王国国名、主法理与法理继承逻辑，修复重复国名、法理丢失和称帝名称错误。
   Reworked kingdom naming, primary de jure titles, and de jure inheritance logic, fixing duplicate kingdom names, lost de jure titles, and incorrect imperial titles upon forming an empire.
2. 完善无地法理、实地法理、行政区授权及朝贡国请封体系。
   Improved the systems for landless de jure titles, territorial de jure titles, administrative region authorization, and tributary title-grant requests.
3. 优化法理窗口及帝国人物浮窗信息显示，补充归属、爵位、行政区和帝国信息。
   Optimized the De Jure interface and imperial character tooltips, adding information on affiliation, titles, administrative regions, and empire status.
4. 调整朝贡、主动臣服、叛乱回归和综合国力判定规则。
   Adjusted the rules for tributary states, voluntary submission, post-rebellion reintegration, and overall national power evaluation.
5. 完善派系中央占比、派系领导人、权臣辅政及太后干政机制。
   Improved faction mechanics, including central influence share, faction leadership, powerful minister regencies, and Empress Dowager regencies.
6. 简易铭牌开关和自适应性能优化, 以及可开关的隐藏后缀按钮
   Added a toggle for simplified nameplates, adaptive performance optimizations, and an option to hide name suffixes.
7. Bug修复。
   Various bug fixes.
8. 模组自更新推送, 英语语言优化
   Added automatic mod update notifications and improved the English localization.
