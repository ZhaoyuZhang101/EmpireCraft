# 更新内容 (Update Features)

2026-10-22 · 0.4.7Beta1

新增战场音效与八系文明背景音乐，镜头拉近时缓缓放起。华夏选定国号重做，可开“姓氏国号”。帝国分裂改为预警、割据、争位内战；文明兴衰与各文明平衡重调。远距离不再宣战，久无接触的战争自动议和；城墙挡军团，水师会登陆。换图层与进图算色更快、全屏翻牌；营建新增绘制道路、连路神力。在线更新改为当场换上新版。修复手机版读不进模组、空位期建不了法理、无游牧处游牧化、色块不刷新、君主即位即老死、读外来旧图崩溃等。

Battle sounds and music for eight civilization families fade in as the camera zooms in. Huaxia dynasty naming reworked, with an optional surname-naming law. Imperial breakups now pass through warnings, secession and succession wars; dynastic cycles and civilization balance are retuned. Distant realms no longer declare war, and wars without contact end in peace; walls block armies and navies land troops. Layer switches and map entry compute colors faster and flip the whole view; new powers draw roads and connect cities. The online updater now installs right away. Fixes: mobile loading, manual titles during interregnums, nomadization with no nomads nearby, stale colors, rulers dying after accession, crashes on other mods' saves.
