# 更新内容 (Update Features)

2026-10-16 · 0.4.6Beta6

开国雄主的统一战争能打到底了：讨伐以吞并为目标，割据者会举国归附，欠饷不再罢兵，开战后兵源逐月征满；开国之君族谱里补记成年子孙，不再身后无嗣。华夏都城宫城画成精细版，近代城区整片发展，高楼光影方向修正；修帝国取色报错。皇子不再随母姓、入母亲的氏族；皇统绝嗣时从真实的诸王支脉里找人继位，世系图沿父祖一路连到先帝，并标出第几位君主。修笔刷改地形等情况下谋划报错导致存不了档；修国名后缀显示成英文键名(如 ZhouFeudalism_hou)。

Founding emperors can now finish their unification wars: campaigns aim to annex, rivals may submit outright, unpaid troops no longer force peace, and levies ramp up over the first months of war. Founders now start with adult sons on record. Huaxia capitals get a detailed palace, modern districts grow as a whole, high-rise lighting is fixed, and an empire color error is fixed. Princes no longer take their mother's surname and clan; when the main line dies out, heirs come from real princely branches, the lineage chart traces them back through their ancestors and numbers each ruler. Fixed saves failing because of broken schemes (e.g. after terrain brushing), and kingdom suffixes showing raw keys such as ZhouFeudalism_hou.
