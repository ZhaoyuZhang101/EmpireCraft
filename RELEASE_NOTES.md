# 更新内容 (Update Features)
---
1. 共和后土地收归国有，地主阶级不再出现。
   After becoming a republic, land is nationalised and the landlord class no longer appears.
2. 文化一旦废除君主制便不能再有皇帝，废君后才成立的君主国会被强制改为现代国家。只有玩家手动回退制度或科技才能恢复。
   Once a culture abolishes the monarchy it can never have emperors again, and monarchies founded afterwards are forcibly modernised. Only a manual rollback of institutions or tech by the player can restore it.
3. 制度树和科技树新增回退功能。
   Added rollback for the institution tree and the tech tree.
4. 新增现代革命：多党共和国中若另一理念占主导，会先举行提前大选，再爆发革命战争。
   Added modern revolutions: when another ideology dominates a multi-party republic, a snap election is held first, then a revolutionary war breaks out.
5. WarBox 的罢工和示威会增加不满与怨气、降低正统值，并可能引发总罢工。
   WarBox strikes and protests now raise dissent and grievance, lower the mandate and can trigger general strikes.
7. 临时政府统一命名为"法理省份名 + 立国理念称呼"，后缀固定，不再变来变去。
   Provisional governments are now named "de jure province name + founding-ideology title", with a fixed suffix that no longer changes.
8. 废除君主制后，倾向统一的政权改用省份名，保守派和军阀仍用王国名。
   After abolition, unification-leaning regimes use province names, while conservatives and warlords keep kingdom names.
9. 宪法新增"国体（立国理念）"条款，国体不再随执政党变动。临时政府施行临时约法。
   The constitution gains a "Nature of state (founding ideology)" clause, so the nature of the state no longer changes with the ruling party. Provisional governments run under a provisional charter.
10. 新增国民教育：国家会在国内传播宪法规定的立国理念。
    Added national education: states spread their constitution's founding ideology among their population.
11. 世界提示中的国名与地图铭牌统一，帝国窗口的名称框也显示完整国号并可直接改名。
    Country names in world messages now match the map nameplates, and the empire window's name box shows the full name and can be used to rename directly.
12. 修复加冕称帝按钮失效：现代国家使用后会直接组建政府，成为正常国家。
    Fixed the Crown as Emperor button; on a modern state it now forms a government directly and makes it a normal state.
13. 现代国家的原版提示改用领导人、政权等措辞，不再出现王室家族提示。
    Vanilla messages for modern states now say "leader" and "state" instead of king and kingdom, and royal-family messages no longer appear.
