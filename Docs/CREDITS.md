# 署名与素材来源 / Credits

## 战声音效 / Battle sounds

`GameResources/sounds/battle/` 下冷兵器的刀剑相击、喊杀、冷兵器环境声，火器的排枪齐射、炮声、火器环境声，用下面这些 CC0 录音加工而成：切段、变调、叠层、按远近压暗、统一响度。加工脚本是 `Tools/SoundForge/battle_forge.py`。其余战声（箭雨、马蹄、近代枪炮、海战）由本模组用代码合成，没有用外部素材。

The cold-steel sword clashes, war cries and ambience, and the black-powder volleys, cannon and ambience in `GameResources/sounds/battle/` are derived from the CC0 recordings below. They were cut, pitch-shifted, layered, distance-filtered and loudness-matched by `Tools/SoundForge/battle_forge.py`. All other battle sounds (arrows, hooves, modern gunfire and shelling, naval) are synthesized by this mod in code and use no external material.

## 文明乐 / Civilization music

`Music/civ/` 下的曲子取自下面这些 CC0 作品（其中华夏一首另附作者条款，见表），由 `Tools/SoundForge/civ_music_forge.py` 统一了响度、做成循环、重新编码成 OGG Vorbis。除了裁掉首尾静音、首尾交叉淡化以便循环，曲子没有别的改动。

The tracks in `Music/civ/` come from the CC0 works below (the Huaxia track has an extra condition from its author, see the table). `Tools/SoundForge/civ_music_forge.py` loudness-matched them, made them loop and re-encoded them as OGG Vorbis. Apart from trimming leading and trailing silence and crossfading the ends so they loop, the music is unchanged.
